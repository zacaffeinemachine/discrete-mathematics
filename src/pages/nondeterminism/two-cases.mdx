In every problem on this page your machine may be nondeterministic. A state may
have several arrows for the same symbol, or none.

<Problem n={1} title="The same at both ends">
Design a nondeterministic machine that accepts exactly the strings of length at
least 2 whose first and last symbols are equal.
<Tests accept={["00", "11", "010", "101", "0110", "1001"]} reject={["", "0", "1", "01", "10", "011", "1100"]} />
<MachineBuilder client:visible nondeterministic language="two-cases/same-at-both-ends" />
</Problem>

<Problem n={2} title="A start or an end">
Design a nondeterministic machine that accepts exactly the strings that begin

<Tests accept={["11", "110", "00", "100", "1100", "0100"]} reject={["", "0", "1", "01", "10", "101", "0110"]} />

<Problem n={3} title="Two or three">
