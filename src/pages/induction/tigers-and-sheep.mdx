On a magic island there live some tigers and one sheep. A tiger can go hungry for ever,
and it would still rather eat the sheep than not. The island is magic, though, and a tiger
that eats the sheep **turns into a sheep itself**. Sheep are content with grass and eat
nothing else.

There are $99$ tigers and one sheep. Is the sheep eaten?

## The rules

1. Every tiger is perfectly rational, every tiger knows that every other tiger is
   perfectly rational, every tiger knows that every tiger knows this, and so on.
2. A tiger values its own life above everything else. Among the outcomes in which it
   survives, it prefers the one in which it has eaten.
3. A tiger that eats the sheep becomes a sheep at that moment, and is then in exactly the
   position the eaten sheep was in.
4. No two tigers eat at the same moment.
